Expert Tips

10 Tips for a Standout Resume

  1. Use action verbs: Started with "Developed", "Implemented", "Led" instead of "Responsible for"
  2. Quantify achievements: Use numbers and percentages to showcase impact
  3. Keep it concise: 1-2 pages for freshers, max 3 for experienced professionals
  4. Tailor for the job: Customize your resume for each position
  5. Use keywords: Include skills mentioned in the job description
  6. Focus on results: Highlight what you achieved, not just duties
  7. Proofread carefully: Even one typo can cost you the job
  8. Use bullet points: Easier to scan and more professional
  9. Add LinkedIn: Include your professional LinkedIn profile URL
  10. Keep format consistent: Same fonts, spacing, and styling throughout
