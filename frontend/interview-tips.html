💡 Interview Tips & Tricks

Expert strategies to ace both technical and HR interviews

Technical Interview Tips

Understand the Problem Before Coding

Technical

Always ask clarifying questions about the problem. Understand constraints, input/output formats, and edge cases before writing a single line of code.

Example: If given "Find two sum", ask: "Are duplicates allowed? What about negative numbers? Should I optimize for space or time?"

Think Aloud During Coding

Technical

Explain your approach and thought process as you code. This helps the interviewer understand your logic and problem-solving approach.

Tip: "I'm thinking of using a hash map to store elements for O(n) lookup time instead of O(n²) brute force approach."

Optimize After You Have a Working Solution

Technical

First, get a working solution. Then discuss optimizations for time and space complexity.

Progression: Brute Force → Working Solution → Optimized Solution

Test Your Code with Examples

Technical

Manually test your solution with provided examples AND edge cases (empty arrays, single elements, duplicates, negative numbers, etc.)

Example: For array problems, test with empty [], single element [1], duplicates [1,1,1]

Behavioral & HR Interview Tips

Use the STAR Method

Behavioral

Situation → Task → Action → Result. Structure all your answers with this framework.

Example: "In my project (Situation), we had performance issues (Task), I optimized the database queries (Action), reducing load time by 60% (Result)."

Prepare for Common HR Questions

Behavioral

Prepare stories for: Your strengths, weaknesses, conflicts, failures, achievements, why this company, where do you see yourself, etc.

Key: Every story should have a learning or achievement outcome

Research the Company Thoroughly

Behavioral

Know the company's mission, recent projects, technologies they use, leadership team, and latest news. Reference them during the interview.

Tip: "I saw your recent announcement about the new product launch and I'm excited about..."

Ask Smart Questions

Behavioral

At the end, ask thoughtful questions about the role, team, growth opportunities, and company culture. This shows genuine interest.

Good questions: "What does a typical day look like?", "How is success measured?", "What are the growth opportunities?"

Day-Before & Day-Of Tips

😴
Get Good Sleep

Sleep 8 hours before the interview. Your brain performs better when well-rested.

👔
Dress Professionally

Dress one level up from the company's typical dress code.

⏰
Arrive Early

Arrive 15 minutes early to compose yourself and avoid stress.

📱
Silence Your Phone

Keep your phone on silent or switched off during the interview.

🤝
Firm Handshake

Give a firm but not aggressive handshake and make eye contact.

😊
Smile & Be Positive

Maintain positive body language and smile genuinely throughout.

Quick Checklist

  • ✅ Researched company and role thoroughly
  • ✅ Prepared 3-4 strong STAR stories
  • ✅ Reviewed core technical concepts
  • ✅ Have questions prepared for interviewer
  • ✅ Know the interviewer's name and role
  • ✅ Tested camera/mic (for video interviews)
  • ✅ Have pen and paper ready
  • ✅ Got good sleep and ate breakfast
